Fichier final : `dist/assets/hero-v2.png`.

Outil : `image_gen`, mode intégré, génération d’une image neuve. Ce visuel publicitaire est une illustration, et non une photographie des locaux ou d’un véhicule de la boutique. Il est signalé comme tel sur le site.

## Prompt utilisé

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: wide website hero photograph for a Tunisian car body spare parts business AF Crash New. Create a cinematic high-end automotive advertising photograph, landscape 1536x1024 or wider. A pristine graphite grey unbranded compact European hatchback, front three-quarter view, positioned entirely in right 60% of frame, nose pointing toward viewer and slightly left. Elegant angular headlights, realistic hood and fender, satin silver-black details. Low camera angle, dramatic precise strip lighting and a very subtle warm orange accent reflection, navy-black industrial studio background and concrete floor. Left 40% is dark clean negative space, nearly black navy, for web text overlay (do not add any text). Premium spare parts catalogue mood, visually photographic, refined, crisp metal, realistic not cartoon, no mechanical exploded diagram. No text, no letters, no watermarks, no visible brand logos, no license plate text, no people. This is an illustrative promotional photo, not a real showroom or stock claim.
